Page 4 — Sessions

The Sessions panel shows all your past AI conversations. It opens as an overlay from the sidebar — click the chat bubble icon in the left sidebar to open it from anywhere in the app.

Opening Sessions​

Click the chat bubble icon in the left sidebar. The Sessions panel slides open as a modal overlay on top of the current page.

Sessions Panel Layout​

The panel contains:

ElementDescription
Search barSearch sessions by keyword — type to filter your conversation history
All buttonShows all sessions (default view)
★ Starred buttonShows only sessions you have starred/favourited
✕ CloseCloses the sessions panel
+ New sessionStart a fresh chat session immediately
Date groupingsSessions are grouped by date (e.g. Yesterday, Last week)
Session rowsEach row shows the opening message of that conversation

Starting a New Session from the Panel​

Click + New session at the top of the panel to start a brand new conversation. This is the same as clicking the + icon in the sidebar directly.

Opening a Past Session​

Click any session row to resume that conversation. The session will open and you can continue asking questions right where you left off.

Searching Sessions​

Type in the Search sessions... box to filter your history by keyword. The list updates instantly as you type — useful for finding a specific analysis you ran previously.

Renaming Sessions with AI​

Intugle provides an intelligent way to rename your sessions using AI-generated suggestions.

How to Rename a Session​

  1. Hover over any session in the Sessions panel
  2. Click the Rename button that appears on the right side
  3. The session name becomes editable in a text field with AI assistance options

Suggest Name with AI​

After clicking Rename, you'll see the session name in an editable text field with three icons on the right:

IconAction
✨ Sparkle iconClick to let AI suggest a descriptive name based on the session content
✓ CheckmarkSave/confirm the new name
✕ X iconCancel editing and keep the original name

The Suggest Name with AI feature analyzes your conversation and generates a meaningful, descriptive name — saving you time and keeping your sessions organized.

Tips​

  • Sessions are workspace-specific — switching workspaces shows only that workspace's sessions
  • Every question you ask in Intugle is automatically saved as a session
  • Use the ★ star filter to quickly access your most important sessions
  • Use Suggest Name with AI to automatically generate descriptive session names
