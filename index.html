Intugle User Guide

Intugle is an AI-powered data platform that lets you query your business data in plain English, build and deploy AI-powered apps, connect data sources, and automate workflows — all without writing code.

Getting Started​

New to Intugle? Follow these steps to get up and running:

StepWhat to DoWhere
1Log in with your email and password (or Google/Microsoft SSO)app.intugle.ai
2Select your workspace or create a new oneWorkspace switcher (top of page)
3Connect your data — upload files or link a databaseData Connections
4Ask a question about your data in plain EnglishChat input on the Homepage
5Review the AI response — charts, tables, and insights appear inlineChat session
6Deploy an app (optional) — publish a ChatApp, Data Product, or Dashboard for your teamDeployed Apps panel on Homepage

Key Concepts​

Before diving in, here are the core concepts you will encounter throughout Intugle:

ConceptWhat It Means
WorkspaceAn isolated environment for a project or team. All data, apps, sessions, and settings belong to a workspace. You can switch between workspaces at any time.
SessionA single conversation thread with the AI. Every question you ask starts or continues a session. Sessions are saved automatically.
AgentsSpecialised AI modules that each handle a different type of task (data analysis, web search, document lookup, etc.). You can toggle them on/off per query.
ScopeA filter that limits which data the AI can access — by domain, table, app, or tag. Useful for focusing queries on a specific part of your business.
Semantic Data GraphA visual map of all your data tables and their relationships, built automatically when you connect data. The AI uses this to understand your schema.
ChatAppA deployed conversational AI app that your team or customers can use to ask questions — without needing access to the full Intugle platform.
Data ProductA structured, processed dataset output from a data pipeline — like a materialised view or ETL result.
DashboardA visual app with charts, tables, and metrics built from your connected data.
Few-ShotsExample question-and-answer pairs that train the AI to respond more accurately for your specific business context.
