## Account Settings

This section lets you update your personal account information. Settings marked **Coming Soon** are not yet available.

| Setting | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal Information** | Update your name and avatar | Coming Soon |
| **Email Settings** | Manage email notification preferences | Available |
| **Change Password** | Update your account password | Available |

### How to Change Your Password

1. Click **Change Password**
2. Enter your current password
3. Enter and confirm your new password
4. Click **Save**

### How to Update Email Settings

1. Click **Email Settings**
2. Toggle notifications on or off as preferred
3. Save your changes

## Workspaces

From the Profile page (and from anywhere via the workspace switcher) you can see and manage all workspaces you have access to:

| Workspace | Description |
|---|---|
| **B2B Technology** ✓ | Currently active workspace |
| **Life Sciences** | Switch to this workspace |
| **Healthcare** | Switch to this workspace |
| **+ Create Workspace** | Add a new workspace |

To switch workspaces, click the workspace name from the switcher dropdown. All data, apps, sessions, and agent configurations are workspace-specific.

---

## Log Out

At the bottom of the Profile & Settings page, click **Log Out** to sign out of your Intugle account.

> Your sessions and work are automatically saved — you will not lose any data when logging out.

:::warning
Logging out ends your current session. Make sure any ongoing analysis is complete before signing out.
:::
